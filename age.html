Compliance

21 and older

Hearthfold Supply sells legal tobacco accessories. We do not sell cigarettes, nicotine vapes, e-liquid, or cannabis. We do not sell to anyone under 21.

Entry to the catalog requires an age confirmation. When card checkout is live, the payment processor’s age and AVS rules apply at checkout. In-person sales require a government photo ID showing the buyer is 21 or older.

We do not ship accessories to buyers we have not age-checked. We do not market to minors.
